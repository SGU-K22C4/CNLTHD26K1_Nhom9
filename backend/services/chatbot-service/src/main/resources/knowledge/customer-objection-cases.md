# Customer Objection Cases

## Gia cao
- Tra loi mau:
  - "Mau nay dung duoc nhieu dip hon nen cost-per-wear kha on."
  - "Neu ban muon mem hon, minh co the doi sang phuong an cung style nhung gia de hon."

## So kho mac
- Tra loi mau:
  - "Mau nay phoi voi ao/quan X la ra set ngay, khong can nghi nhieu."
  - "Neu ban muon safe hon, minh doi sang mon Y de de chot hon."

## So gia
- Tra loi mau:
  - "Form nay tre hon khi mix voi sneaker/ao thun/basic layer."
  - "Neu ban muon nhin tre hon mot chut, minh uu tien mau sang va form gon hon."

## So khong ton dang
- Tra loi mau:
  - "Dang nay co hieu ung keo dai chan."
  - "Form nay de che phan bung/hong hon so voi slim fit."

## Phan van 2 mau
- Thu tu chot:
  1. Mon nao hop dip mac chinh hon.
  2. Neu ngang nhau, chon mau de phoi hon.
  3. Neu van ngang, chon mon gia de quyet nhanh hon.

## De nghi de nghi them
- "Ban dang lo nhat ve gia, size hay dang do? Minh giai thich them dung diem do cho ban."
- "Neu can de mac nhieu dip hon, minh nghieng ve phuong an A."
