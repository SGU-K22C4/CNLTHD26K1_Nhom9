# Customer Personas

## Core audience

Modimal serves urban customers from 22-38 years old who want clothing that is wearable, modern, polished, and easy to style across work and daily life.

Default style priority when information is limited:

1. Minimalist basic
2. Smart casual
3. Contemporary trend

The chatbot should sound like a young stylist with taste: warm, practical, clear, and never pushy.

## Persona 1 - Office Worker

Typical needs:

- office outfits
- polished but not too formal
- repeatable weekday styling
- easy coordination through the week

Priority:

- flattering silhouette
- safe choices
- easy styling

Strong items:

- white shirts
- blouses
- black trousers
- blazers
- midi skirts
- simple dresses

## Persona 2 - Young Professional

Typical needs:

- stylish outfits
- trend-aware choices
- pieces that work for both work and going out

Priority:

- visual impact
- current trend
- personality

Strong items:

- oversized blazers
- wide-leg trousers
- straight jeans
- modern dresses
- thin knitwear

## Persona 3 - Casual Shopper

Typical needs:

- daily wear
- weekend coffee or travel outfits
- comfort and easy styling

Priority:

- comfort
- price-value balance
- low-effort coordination

Strong items:

- basic tees
- jeans
- shorts
- cardigans
- simple dresses
- linen or cotton shirts

## Occasion mapping

- office worker -> office, meetings, client visits
- young professional -> office, coffee, social events, travel
- casual shopper -> daily wear, weekend, travel

## Voice rules

Do:

- explain why an item fits the need
- keep the reply short and useful
- guide the customer toward easier decisions

Do not:

- sound like a hard-sell salesperson
- recite technical fabric specs unless relevant
- exaggerate with luxury language
