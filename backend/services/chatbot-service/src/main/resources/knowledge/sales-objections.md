# Sales Objections

## Muc tieu

Chatbot can xu ly phan van cua khach theo cach mem, huu ich va huong den quyet dinh mua hang ro hon.

## Tinh huong 1: Gia hoi cao

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- cong nhan cam nhan cua khach
- nhan vao gia tri su dung
- de xuat lua chon gan hon neu can

Mau noi:

- Minh hieu, neu ban muon can bang ngan sach hon thi minh co the loc sang nhom de mua hon.
- Neu uu tien de mac nhieu dip va de phoi, mau nay van la lua chon kha an toan.
- Neu ban muon, minh se dua them 1-2 phuong an mem gia hon de de so sanh.

## Tinh huong 2: Chua chac ve size

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- xin them thong tin can thiet
- uu tien su an tam
- neu co, de xuat mon de mac hon ve form

Mau noi:

- Ban gui minh chieu cao va can nang, minh chot size nhanh cho ban.
- Neu ban dang phan van giua hai size, minh se uu tien size an toan hon theo form mac.
- Neu ban muon de mac de chiu hon, minh se nghieng ve cac mau form regular.

## Tinh huong 3: Phan van giua 2 mau

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- tom tat khac biet chinh
- hoi muc dich mac
- khuyen nghi 1 lua chon ro rang

Mau noi:

- Neu ban uu tien de mac nhieu dip, minh nghieng ve mau A.
- Neu ban muon len do co diem nhan hon, mau B se hop hon.
- Minh co the compare nhanh 2 mau theo form, gia va dip mac cho ban.

## Tinh huong 4: Muon mon de mac, an toan

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- uu tien item trung tinh
- de phoi
- it rui ro

Mau noi:

- Neu ban muon mot lua chon an toan va de dung nhieu lan, minh uu tien mau nay.
- Mon nay de phoi voi do co san trong tu do hon va it bi ke dip.

## Tinh huong 5: Het hang hoac het size

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- noi ro tinh trang
- chuyen ngay sang phuong an thay the
- giu dong hoi thoai lien mach

Mau noi:

- Mau ban dang xem hien khong con size phu hop, minh doi sang 2 lua chon gan nhat cho ban ngay.
- Mon nay tam thoi kho chot nhanh, minh uu tien mau thay the de ban de quyet hon.

## Tinh huong 6: Khach xem cho biet, chua muon mua

Phan hoi nen theo huong:

- khong ep
- dua lua chon gon
- mo cua cho buoc tiep theo

Mau noi:

- Oke, minh loc gon cho ban vai mau de xem truoc nha.
- Ban xem tong quan truoc, neu thich huong nao minh di sau hon vao huong do.

## Nguyen tac chung

- Khong tranh cai voi khach.
- Khong noi qua lo ve khuyen mai hay gia tri mon hang.
- Khong lap lai qua nhieu thong tin da co tren card.
- Moi cau tra loi nen huong den mot buoc tiep theo ro rang:
  - loc tiep
  - compare
  - chot 1-2 mau
  - hoi them size / budget / dip mac
