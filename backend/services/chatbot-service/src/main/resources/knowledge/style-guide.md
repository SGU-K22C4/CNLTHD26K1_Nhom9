# Style Guide

## Muc tieu

Tai lieu nay giup chatbot hieu logic tu van theo dip mac va phong cach,
de phan text nghe giong mot stylist ban hang, khong chi la cong cu tim kiem.

## Nhom dip mac

### Di lam

Uu tien:

- thanh lich
- gon gang
- de phoi lap lai
- mau trung tinh hoac de chot

Nhom mon thuong hop:

- ao so mi
- quan ong dung
- chan vay midi
- dam don sac
- ao khoac nhe

### Di choi

Uu tien:

- thoai mai
- de van dong
- tre trung
- mix de dang

Nhom mon thuong hop:

- ao thun
- so mi casual
- jean
- chan vay casual
- ao khoac mem

### Di tiec / su kien

Uu tien:

- co diem nhan
- form dep
- tong the chi chu
- de tao hinh anh chinh chu hoac noi bat

Nhom mon thuong hop:

- dam
- chan vay co diem nhan
- ao kieu
- ao khoac chinh form

### Du lich

Uu tien:

- de mac
- de phoi
- de di chuyen
- hop thoi tiet

Nhom mon thuong hop:

- ao thun
- so mi mong
- quan short hoac chan vay de di chuyen
- ao khoac nhe

## Nhom phong cach

### Basic / minimal

Dac diem:

- it chi tiet
- de mac lai nhieu lan
- de phoi voi nhau
- hop user muon an toan

### Smart casual

Dac diem:

- gon gang nhung khong qua cung
- hop di lam, di gap go, di cafe
- nen uu tien item de len form

### Feminine / elegant

Dac diem:

- mem, co do roi
- ton su thanh lich
- hop tiep khach, hen ho, du tiec nhe

### Sporty / relaxed

Dac diem:

- thoai mai
- de hoat dong
- hop di choi va mac hang ngay

## Quy tac phoi tong mau

Neu user chua ro mau:

- uu tien den, trang, kem, be, navy, xam

Neu user muon an toan:

- ton trung tinh
- ton mau de phoi lap lai

Neu user muon noi bat:

- chi nen co 1 diem nhan ro
- phan con lai giu tong mau de can bang

## Quy tac chon form

Neu user chua ro form:

- di lam: regular fit, gon gang, vua van
- di choi: co the relaxed hoac oversized nhe
- di tiec: uu tien len form dep, dung vai va eo

## Quy tac budget

- budget thap: uu tien mon de mac nhieu dip
- budget tam trung: uu tien mon can bang giua tinh ung dung va diem nhan
- budget cao hon: co the uu tien chat lieu, form va kha nang len outfit dep hon

## Quy tac dien dat

Chatbot nen noi theo kieu:

- Mau nay hop di lam vi tong the gon va de phoi.
- Mau nay de mac nhieu dip hon, nhat la neu ban thich phong cach toi gian.
- Neu ban muon mot lua chon an toan va de chot nhanh, minh nghieng ve mau nay.
